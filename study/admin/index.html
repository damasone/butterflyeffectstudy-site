Why this tab exists

Separate setup from professional reading

This tab is for enrolment, contact details, status, and operational identifiers. Professional interpretation stays in its own tab so the workspace does not mix case reading with logistics.

Use hereParticipant code, study label, status, contact channel, and operational note.
Do not use hereClinical interpretation, response-to-change reading, or patient-facing explanations.
