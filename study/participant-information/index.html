Study

What the project is studying

Research question

Butterfly Effect examines whether repeated sleep, overnight physiology, movement, context and self-reported state data can support careful within-person pattern analysis.

What participation involves

Participants may complete repeated questionnaires and contribute compatible sleep, movement or physiology summaries. Relevant changes can be recorded as context events.

No guaranteed benefit

A participant may receive a descriptive research summary, but participation may produce no direct personal benefit and does not replace healthcare.

Data

What is collected and why

The project applies data minimisation and separates account identity from analytical records wherever practical.

Collected data

Account identifiers, questionnaire answers, optional notes, sleep and physiology summaries, movement-related measures, context events and derived research features.

Purpose

Longitudinal research, quality control, transparent method development, validation, participant research summaries and bounded scientific review.

Legal basis

Health-related study data are processed with explicit participant consent. The system records the notice version, time and proof attached to that consent.

Control

Access, retention and participant rights

Access

Access is limited to the project lead and specifically authorised research, technical, clinical or academic collaborators when required for the stated purpose. Technical providers may support authentication, database, storage and hosting.

Retention

Identifiable data are retained for the active study and review period. A deidentified research and audit record may be kept for up to five years after study closure, subject to applicable law, ethics review, withdrawal handling or institutional agreement.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, restriction, portability or deletion where applicable, object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to the competent data-protection authority.

Withdrawal

Withdrawal prevents new submissions. Existing-data requests are handled according to applicable legal, scientific-integrity and deletion obligations. Use the official project contact pathway.

Burden and limits

What to consider before consenting

Time and discomfort

Repeated questionnaires take time, and reflecting on sleep or daily state may occasionally feel uncomfortable.

Measurement limits

Consumer-device measurements can be incomplete or inaccurate. Research outputs retain visible quality and uncertainty limits.

Questions

Participants should ask any questions they need before accepting. Consent is an active choice, not a condition hidden inside account creation.
