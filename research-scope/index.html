Research scope

Ambitious direction, strict current boundaries.

Butterfly Effect explores longitudinal signal interpretation. It may support future clinical research pathways, but the current project does not make diagnostic, treatment, prevention, disease risk, or clinical decision claims.

Claim boundary

  • Can studyWithin-person changes, signal reliability, and reviewable longitudinal patterns.
  • Can prepareFuture clinical validation pathways and disease-specific study designs.
  • Cannot claim todayDiagnosis, treatment, disease risk, or replacement for clinical evaluation.

Current scope

What the system can responsibly say today.

Current outputs are research signals for review. They can describe quality, uncertainty, route status, evidence anchors, and whether a pattern is strong enough to be interpreted inside its intended scope.

Signal interpretation

Repeated sleep, physiology, movement, context, and self report patterns can be routed through governed model layers.

Confidence boundaries

Route stability, feature dependency, data quality, uncertainty, and validation strength can limit how much confidence an output receives.

Professional review

The project can produce structured research summaries that keep evidence and limitations attached for discussion with qualified professionals.

Not claimed today

The current public surface is deliberately conservative.

This boundary protects the project scientifically and ethically while it prepares stronger validation routes.

Current limits

No current diagnosis, medical advice, treatment recommendation, disease risk prediction, cognitive impairment claim, Alzheimer or dementia prediction, brain injury assessment, rehabilitation prescription, or replacement for clinical evaluation is made by this website or project surface.
