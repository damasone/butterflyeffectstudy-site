For academic, clinical, methodological, or institutional conversations.

Butterfly Effect is currently seeking serious research feedback, methodological guidance, validation advice, and exploratory collaboration with people working across sleep science, digital health, neuroscience, clinical research, psychology, data science, and longitudinal health data.

Best fit

Useful conversations are specific.

The project is most interested in feedback that improves scientific design, data quality, validation standards, ethical framing, or future pilot-study pathways.

Academic and research teams

Study design, external validation, clinical endpoints, longitudinal modelling, and evidence standards.

Clinical professionals

Claim boundaries, participant-facing interpretation, professional review summaries, and safe future clinical framing.

Data and digital health groups

Wearable data quality, sensor fusion, model reliability, route stability, and transparent reporting.

How to reach the project

If you arrived here through an email or institutional introduction, the best contact route is to reply in that same thread with the area you would like to discuss. Please do not send sensitive personal medical information through general contact messages.
